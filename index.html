Reading Between the Brushstrokes

a reintroduction of the myths and figures behind these iconic paintings

While Greek mythology is still referenced and depicted in popular culture today, many of the stories have become misunderstood or eclipsed by retellings and reimaginings. Many can stand before a painting of Medusa and recognize her for who she is but they may only understand her from the context of a modern film, TV show, or book; her story and background lost to the modern age. A modern viewer may admire the drama of a scene without knowing what the drama is about.

This exhibition reindroduces the context for the viewer. Each painting is presented with the Getty Museum's own description but also includes interactive points that identify figures and places within a scene, explaining their background and their purpose within it. Viewers are invited to explore each work and the story within.

All artwork images and museum information are drawn from The Getty Museum's open data collection and all additional information is drawn from britannica.com, theoi.com and, greekmythology.com.
